process - it is a program under execution 

in operating system, there are two types of scheduling
1. Preemptive
2. Non preemptive

1. Preemptive multiprogramming operating system - in this OS, a process dispatched to CPU can be preempted by the OS to go back to main memory in order to execute or dispatch another process of higher priority by CPU
2. Non preemptive multiprogramming operating system - in this OS, a process under execution regardless of priority or time consumed remains under execution until terminated
