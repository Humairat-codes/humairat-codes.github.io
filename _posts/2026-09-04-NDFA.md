## DFA - Deterministic Finite Automata
- Given current state, we know what the next state will be
- It has only one Unique Next State
- No choices or randomness

 ![DFA](/assets/dfavsnfa1.png)

---

## NFA - Non-Deterministic Finite Automata
- Given current state, there could be multiple next states
- Next state maybe chosen at random
- or all next states may be chosen in paralle
- There is randomness

 ![NFA](/assets/dfavsnfa2.png)
