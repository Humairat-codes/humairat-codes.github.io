The **bit** is the smallest unit of data (a `0` or a `1`). Bits are grouped into bytes, and bytes into larger units.

## Conversion table

| Unit | Equals | In bytes |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 Byte | 8 bits | 2⁰ |
| 1 KB (Kilobyte) | 1024 Bytes | 2¹⁰ |
| 1 MB (Megabyte) | 1024 KB | 2²⁰ |
| 1 GB (Gigabyte) | 1024 MB | 2³⁰ |
| 1 TB (Terabyte) | 1024 GB | 2⁴⁰ |

Each step up multiplies by **1024 (2¹⁰)**.

> **Note for networking:** Data *sizes* use bytes (KB, MB, GB), but data *transfer speeds* are measured in **bits** per second (Kbps, Mbps, Gbps). Network speeds usually use powers of 10 (1 Mbps = 1,000,000 bits/s), not 1024. So a 100 Mbps connection transfers at most about 12.5 MB per second (100 ÷ 8).
